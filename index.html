About

I am a postdoctoral fellow in the RPI-Bio team at ISIR (Sorbonne University). My research focuses on modeling, simulation, and control of soft/continuum robots and medical robotic systems, with an emphasis on geometrically nonlinear mechanics, contact interactions, and optimization-based methods.

  • Languages: Chinese (native), French (fluent), English (fluent).
  • Keywords: Cosserat rods, rigid–flexible dynamics, contact mechanics, numerical optimization, control, medical robotics.

Research

Geometric modeling

Geometrically consistent modeling of soft/slender robots (Cosserat rods) and nonlinear deformable bodies.

Contact & interaction

Efficient contact mechanics modeling for elastic bodies and robot–environment interaction processes.

Control & optimization

Model-based controllers (Jacobian, optimal control, MPC) and learning-augmented methods for high-DOF systems.

Projects

RHU ICELAND (2024–)

Image-based robotic solution for transcatheter mitral annuloplasty via the femoral artery. I lead the development of a digital twin of the surgical process and contribute to modeling and control of the ICELAND robot.

ANR ROBOCOP (2020–2024)

Active cochlear implant: physical modeling of the implantation device, simulation of human–robot interaction during insertion, and design of control algorithms to assist/automate the implantation process.

ANR COSSEROOTS (2021–2023)

Dynamic modeling and model-based control of continuum robots, with applications to exploration and medical domains.
