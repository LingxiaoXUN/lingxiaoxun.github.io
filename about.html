Biography

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the Institut des Systèmes Intelligents et de Robotique (ISIR), Sorbonne University, where I joined the RPI-Bio team in April 2024.

Before that, I completed my PhD at Inria Lille (CRIStAL laboratory), under the supervision of Alexandre Kruszewski and Gang Zheng. This PhD was awarded the best PhD thesis award by the CNRS research group on robotics for the year 2024.

Prior to my PhD, I obtained an engineering degree in robotics from Arts et Métiers ParisTech.

  • Languages: Chinese (native), French (fluent), English (fluent).
  • Keywords: Rigid–flexible dynamics, contact mechanics, numerical optimization, control, medical robotics.
